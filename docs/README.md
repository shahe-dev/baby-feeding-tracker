# Research documents

The tracker uses four research families: **WHO 2023, LEAP and follow-ups, Scandinavian/Nordic research, and New Zealand BLISS**.

- [Feeding research for 12–23 months](feeding-research-12-23-months.md) is the current evidence review. It links primary sources, separates guideline recommendations from trial protocols and authored meal ideas, and identifies claims that needed correcting.
- [Original infant-feeding synthesis](revised-evidence-based-feeding-schedule.md) is an **unchanged archive** of the document from [commit 1c36083](https://github.com/shahe-dev/baby-feeding-tracker/blob/1c36083a45daa525a2aefffb448848a14eeb2c57/docs/revised-evidence-based-feeding-schedule.md). Its preservation records the project's research history; it does not validate every assertion or make its schedule a tested toddler diet.

The archived synthesis includes overstatements about allergen frequency, iron deficiency prevention, fat intake and fish-associated allergy outcomes. The current review explains each correction. Its title and original wording remain intact so that historical content is distinguishable from subsequent interpretation.

The repository's original synthesis does not include the complete underlying bibliography or selected papers. WHO, LEAP and BLISS can be identified as research programmes. ABIS and the Western Sweden fish studies are documented matches for the Scandinavian claims, with different study populations and outcomes. NNR2023, OTIS and PreventADALL were newly identified within the permitted Nordic family; they are not claimed to be the exact documents originally selected.

Meal plans in the app are practical adaptations of this evidence. Published research does not establish those exact weekly menus, individual portions, or a universal exposure target for every allergen.
